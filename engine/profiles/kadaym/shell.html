Jurah Makor

The imperial heartland around Mahanzk. Eighteen systems follow the regional chart, with Varkesh Abyss and Vezhkar Pulsar in its outer reaches. Together with adjacent Jurah Lendor it forms the Federation’s central seat. Auvik and Dovrel occupy the outer heartland.

About this chart

This atlas assembles the known territory of the United Federation of Kadaym, using current Codex articles and regional map images. Campaign reference: 134 CE. Sources consulted 30 October 2026.

How to use the atlas

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Click inside a region to highlight it at the current zoom. Faint names repeat inside the regional boundaries. Select a star for its dossier; double-click its marker to focus its system; select a location’s name to open its Codex page. Search spans all regions, including unplotted worlds. Use + / − to zoom, arrow keys to pan, and 0 to fit the current region when the chart has keyboard focus.

Animated system views

Each selected system displays its named worlds in a compact orbital diagram. Documented orbital positions determine the actual numbered ring: a third planet is drawn on ring 3, with two smaller planets inward of it. Dim, unnamed planets fill gaps in the schematic diagram. The orbit-order strip reads from inner to outer. Asterisks mark named worlds whose orbital position is unspecified. Planetary views show the natural satellite counts recorded in the planet articles, including named moons and catalogue designations for unnamed moons. Only confirmed inhabited moons appear in solar-system view; Zelenzk remains visible around Mahanzk in both views. Select a numbered body to inspect it, or its name to open the Codex. Pause / Play controls the motion, and reduced-motion preferences are respected. Orbital positions shown as numbers come from planet infoboxes; orbital sizes, inclinations, speeds, phases and the ordering of worlds without a known orbital position are schematic. Stations use distinct symbols for Civil Engineering, Habitation, Military, Weaponry, Industry and Other; documented sites with no specialist category are grouped under Other and labelled as unspecified. Existing station and colony host orbits are not inferred: they appear in an unlocated row. Destroyed Salem Cross remains a historical site. Use Focus system to expand the solar system into the main chart, filter station categories, and inspect object dossiers. Return to region (or Escape) restores the regional chart. All station names link to their matching or expected Codex article titles. Reported binary primaries orbit their shared center. Select the central star or either binary member to inspect its own dossier; its name opens its Codex article. Use Focus planet & satellites to inspect a named world’s local moons and stations, and Return to system to step back. Faint asteroid belts can be hidden with the Belts control. Belt placement and stellar motion are schematic. Pulsar emission ribbons twist around a fixed polar axis; magnetars show a much gentler, slower depth shift in their ribbons and magnetic fields. The cataclysmic variable is an accreting white dwarf and donor pair. Stellar effects are slowed schematic illustrations, not measured rotation or outburst periods.

What the map establishes

The Makor chart, Lendor chart and Hinterlands chart establish local system arrangements and listed worlds. This atlas preserves their relative layouts. System points represent whole systems; worlds are listed beneath them and in dossiers.

Spacing between regions and territorial outlines are schematic. The remote frontier is a separate inset. There is no measured navigational scale, route network or implied travel time. The 224-lightyear figure is the Federation’s published territorial span, not a pixel-to-distance conversion. Faint background stars are decoration and do not represent additional named systems.

Colors distinguish Federation systems, neighboring/neutral systems, and hazards/unresolved designations. They do not reproduce an undocumented color legend from the source images. An unnamed world slot does not mean a system is uninhabited. “Named worlds only” includes systems with named stations or other sites.

Regional ownership

Valteo is a compact acquired region of the Federation, centered on Xai and Histekka. Four of its five systems belonged to the former Oskalen Empire. Tserazk Makai, Uldrei Vahr and Kezhrava are Kurzenzka sectors. Red hatching marks the occupied territory. Each KRZ marker shows its local infestation tier. The Drayson Hinterlands belong to the Elysian Collective, with the Kadaymian foothold on Prathia in Kevak and independent Montressor at the rimward edge.

Beyond the chart

Ferryman’s Rest remains unplotted because its location is classified. Open MAKOR-9* from the Unplotted register to inspect its black hole and Ferryman’s Rest. The former unplotted settlements now appear under their host systems. The original unlocated KRZ records remain under Unplotted. The three charted Kurzenzka sectors use KRZ designations. At KRZ-147 in Tserazk Makai, the Nefandum Nefmaat follows a very slow orbit around a blackened star. The article describes 22 systems in the Hinterlands; the image shows 23 labels including independent Montressor.

This is a selective atlas: the Codex’s 200+ controlled worlds, thousands of stations and 31,223 stars are not all individually named. All named locations link to their matching articles or expected article titles, including pages that have not yet been written.
