Reading the chart

Drag or pinch to navigate. Choose a chart with the dropdown. Click open territory, or focus its boundary and press Enter, to highlight a region without moving the camera. Overview labels appear at 150% and planet sublabels at 220%; a highlighted region uses 120% and 150%. Regional charts use 70% and 90% for the highlighted region, 90% and 135% for neighbors. If the map’s shorter dimension is below 520 pixels, thresholds increase proportionally to preserve legibility. Hover over the zoom percentage for the current thresholds. The Labels control also hides region inscriptions. Select a symbol for its dossier, or double-click a system or planet to enter its chart. Names link to the Codex. Collapse either side panel for more chart space. Escape steps back, arrow keys pan the focused map, +/− zoom and 0 fits the region.

Source and scope

The Codex identifies the Union’s 41 systems, approximately 228 billion people, its capital New Avendon, and its location in the Iryddis Region. Atlas divisions, system positions and planetary orbits follow the proposed IULR master register v3. Station counts follow this atlas edition. Avedon is the ruined founding site tentatively placed on Vaaskel, and Ysh Zaħah is the Turahk homeworld tentatively placed in the Zhaur system.

Orbital diagrams

All 265 primary planetary slots are catalogued. The 168 without proper names use a dimmer symbol. Moons use provisional catalogue designations. Planetary charts show their full satellite families. Close pairs host circumbinary planets. Trinaries add a distant companion C, shown separately. Spacing, sizes and speeds are schematic.

TORA shroud

Each system has one or two proposed arrays, included in its station total. Flowing blue waves at the edges mark engineered cloaking. Nearby telescopes and exceptional energetic objects limit concealment. The arrays are shown in the lower-left register beyond the scale of the planetary orbits. This edition charts 249 ordinary stations plus 59 distant arrays. Each system diagram shows one or two major facilities. Planetary diagrams show each host’s full station inventory.
